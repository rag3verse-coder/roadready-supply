PSI Finder

Your tires have one right number — and it is NOT the number printed on the tire sidewall. That is the tire's maximum, not your car's recommendation. Your real number lives on the driver's door jamb sticker.

Where the real number lives

Open the driver's door and look at the sticker on the door jamb (sometimes inside the fuel door or glove box). It lists the front and rear PSI your car was engineered for. Check pressure when tires are cold — before driving, or at least 3 hours after parking — because driving heats tires and raises the reading.

Typical ranges only — your door jamb sticker overrules everything on this page. Motorcycles vary the most by model and load, so the sticker (or owner's manual) is the only number to trust there.
