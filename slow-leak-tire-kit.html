A slow leak is the most common tire problem and the easiest to ignore: the tire looks fine, the light comes on every few days, you top it off and forget it. That cycle is the warning. A tire that needs air weekly has a reason — a nail, a valve issue, a bead leak, or a puncture that sealed partially. Topping off buys safe miles to a shop; it does not fix the cause, and the longer the cycle runs, the closer it gets to a blowout.

How to handle it

  1. Note the pattern: same tire, how often, how much air.
  2. Top off to the door-jamb number with the cutoff set, never by feel.
  3. Look and listen at the tire while it fills, and check the valve cap and tread for a nail.
  4. Book the shop visit on a schedule, not on the day it finally goes flat. If pressure falls within minutes, stop and call for help.
